# Cardiovascular Disease Data Visualization

An end-to-end data visualization project analyzing cardiovascular disease risk factors — built with **Python (Jupyter Notebook)** and **Tableau**, and presented as a stakeholder-driven analytical report.

---

## Project Overview

Cardiovascular disease (CVD) remains one of the leading causes of death worldwide. This project explores a cleaned patient-level dataset to uncover **who is most at risk and why**, and translates the findings into recommendations for three real-world stakeholders:

- **Hospital Management** — prevalence patterns across age and BMI groups
- **Cardiologists** — clinical risk indicators (blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose)
- **Public Health Department** — lifestyle risk factors and preventive program priorities

---

## Key Insights

- Nearly **49% of patients** in the dataset have cardiovascular disease.
- Disease prevalence rises steadily with age, peaking in the **50–59** age group.
- **High cholesterol** and **physical inactivity** are the most common risk factors.
- Elevated **BMI**, **blood pressure**, and **cholesterol** are the strongest predictors of CVD.
- Patients with **systolic BP > 140 mmHg** and **diastolic BP > 90 mmHg** are flagged as highest priority for early intervention.
